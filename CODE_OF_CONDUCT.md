# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in qr2 to be useful and safe for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code and kind to people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim matters.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Give credit for prior work and reference vectors.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to keep the project focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith reports.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer through GitHub at <https://github.com/h4nxxlmao>. Reports are reviewed as promptly as possible. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close contributions, or block accounts when needed.
